jax-Ka Model Architecture

A coordinate model predicts each site's intrinsic pKa and the site–site coupling matrix. jax-Ka's titration solver turns those into curves. The model runs twice with the same weights, once on the complex and once on each partner alone. Subtracting the two passes gives ΔpKa per site, and the charge difference gives the pH dependence of binding.

Forward pass

One encoder, two passes, subtract

Why siamese

The same weights run on both states and the outputs are subtracted. The gradient of the difference is the difference of gradients, so any error the encoder makes identically in both states adds nothing to the loss. Error cancellation becomes a property of training.

Partner presence is input content only

The free state is the identical structure with the partner's atoms removed. There is no state flag and no extra pathway. Sanity check, used as a diagnostic and never as a loss: ΔpKa(A→AB) must equal −ΔpKa(AB→A). Any drift means a bug.

Encoder fθ

Sparse attention from titratable sites

Representation

  • Invariant features in local N–CA–C frames. No equivariant vector channels; gradients still reach coordinates through frame construction.
  • Sparse radius-graph attention with queries only at titratable sites.
  • Soft sequence P[N,20] end to end. No argmax and no rotamer search.
  • Side-chain channel plus a learned “absent” embedding, so masked side chains are distinguishable from zero-valued features (from 04).
  • Raw coordinates are featurised on the fly. Nothing heavy is precomputed except for the CatBoost baseline.

Smoothness checklist

  • Cosine or sigmoid switching over a 2 Å window. No hard distance cutoffs.
  • Fixed-k neighbours from a detached index computation; smooth weights carry the gradient.
  • sqrt(r² + ε) everywhere. Never norm() at zero.
  • Gram–Schmidt frame construction guarded against degenerate N–CA–C triples.
  • SiLU or GELU, not ReLU, to keep second derivatives for design.
  • fp32. The pair matrix is distance-dependent, and the expanded-form distance trick cancels catastrophically in bf16.
Output heads

Bounded residuals on physics

intrinsic = model_pka[restype] + SCALE * tanh(head_i(h))      # starts at "no shift"
W_raw     = debye_huckel_baseline(coords, I) * (1 + head_w(h_pair))
W         = 0.5 * (W_raw + W_raw.T); W = W.at[diag].set(0)

Intrinsic head, one value per site

A shift added to the residue type's model pKa. The tanh caps it at ±SCALE. A head that outputs near zero returns the model value, so an untrained network starts in a physically sane place.

Pair head, one value per site pair

A Debye–Hückel baseline supplies the distance decay and ionic-strength screening. The network learns only the relative deviation from it. The result is symmetrised and the diagonal is zeroed. Gradients stay small and well-scaled.

Solver

jax-Ka’s relaxation, with three changes

ChangeCurrent jax-Ka (model.py)Planned
Coupling Damped mean-field relaxation over all sites: h ← h + 0.35·(σ(ln10·(pKint − pH − field(h))) − h), 64 steps. Exact enumeration inside clusters of ≤ 10–12 sites, mean-field between clusters.
Gradient Reverse mode through the unrolled fori_loop; memory grows with the step count. Implicit differentiation of the fixed point with lax.custom_root or a custom VJP: dx/dθ = (I − ∂F/∂x)⁻¹ ∂F/∂θ, solved by CG with a small Tikhonov term because the system is ill-conditioned near strongly coupled transitions. Constant memory.
Midpoint Batched kernels (grid_pka_kernel) interpolate the 73-point grid, which kinks the gradient when the crossing moves between intervals. TitrationModel.pka already bisects with a custom JVP. Implicit root hi(pKai) = ½ as the training path.

Extension · experiment 04

Backbone-only mode from the same checkpoint

MSE under random side-chain masking converges to the conditional mean over the implied rotamer distribution. Masking during training therefore gives the right target without a new labelling campaign. Repacked ensemble labels only reduce variance.

Scope of the claim

What this architecture can and cannot say

  • Titration and binding linkage from a fixed conformation. pH-dependent conformational change is out of scope; histidine switches and endosomal release are expected failures.
  • The teacher’s internal dielectric of 15 compresses shifts toward model values, so the labels themselves under-predict large shifts.
  • For mutations, the claim is the ranking and direction of change in pH dependence, with one frozen side-chain placement per substitution and no repacking. The model does not predict ΔΔGbind.
  • Backbone-only mode returns a rotamer-averaged pKa. It cannot resolve cases where one rotamer is mechanistically essential, and buried histidines may be smoothed.
