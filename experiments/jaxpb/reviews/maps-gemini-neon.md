### 1. Causes of the A40 Slowdown (+2.34 s/state Pallas 20, +2.21 s/state XLA 12)

Pre-change A40 maps ([profile2.json](results/profile2.json#L538-L562)) was **3.40 s/state**: `reach_mask` 0.84 s (24.6%), `cell_tables` 0.70 s (20.5%), atom queries (`mid`+`contact`+`ion`+`cull`+`b_atom`+`bproj_c`) 1.00 s (29.4%), probe-projection queries 0.32 s (9.4%), other/region_probes 0.54 s (16.0%).

Dropping the dense `[ncell^3, K]` materialization in [maps.py:67-71](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L67-L71) avoids writing ~11.7 GB/batch (~65 GB/state) of probe tables, **saving an estimated ~0.15–0.25 s/state**. The net **+2.34 s/state** regression therefore implies **~+2.50–2.60 s/state gross** from six regressions, ranked below (**all costs are estimates**):

1. **CSR 32-slot slab `fori_loop` for `Kp = 768` probe queries — est. `+0.60 to +1.00 s/state`** ([maps.py:134-158](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L134-L158), [_q_chunk:108-110](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L108-L110), [build_maps_b:538](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L538))
   - **Batched dynamic trip count**: `nslab = (jnp.max(count) + 31) // 32` ([maps.py:139](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L139)) is traced inside `jax.vmap` ([maps.py:354](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L354)). `vmap(fori_loop)` lowers to a `while_loop` stopping only on `jnp.any(k < nslab)` with masked carry updates, so **all 16 sites** execute `max_b(nslab[b])` over `16 × 809 × 27 = 349,488` cell lookups. Because dense SAS-arc probe cells reach ~300–650 probes (`Kp` was raised 576→768), `nslab` is typically **10–21** sequential iterations per chunk even though >85% of the 27 neighbour cells finish in 1–2 slabs.
   - **Per-slab kernel/reduction overhead & no cross-slab fusion**: Every slab runs a separate `argmin(d, axis=1)` over `27 × 32 = 864` candidates, **two** `take_along_axis` calls ([maps.py:151-153](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L151-L153)), `rank` arithmetic, 3× `jnp.where` merges, outer `vmap` state-masking, and a `while_loop` exit reduction (~1,500–3,000 inner slab iterations/state vs single fused reduction in baseline).
   - **Double indirection + unpredicated `order` gathers**: `order[jnp.minimum(kk, ...)]` ([maps.py:147](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L147)) gathers from `[16, NP_C[c]]` (`100–201 MB` for c2–c3, 17–33× A40's 6 MB L2) *before* masking by `kk < en`, loading unrelated indices for empty slots; because `idx` is live across `argmin` for `take_along_axis(idx, a)` ([maps.py:153](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L153)), XLA must materialize `idx` (45 MB/slab) or recompute the double gather.
   - **26% smaller outer chunk**: `_q_chunk(1, 768, 16) = 809` vs baseline `1,024`.

2. **Atom queries (`Ka = 24`): 6× smaller query chunks + CSR double-indirection — est. `+0.60 to +1.00 s/state`** ([maps.py:108-110](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L108-L110), [159-162](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L159-L162), [356-374](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L356-L374), [424](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L424), [462-463](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L462-L463))
   - **6× chunk shrinkage (`+0.35 to +0.55 s/state`, est.)**: `GPU_QUERY_BYTES = 512 MiB` divided by `B=16` and an overly conservative `48 B/candidate` (CPU materialization model) drops atom `reach=1` chunk from **`6,472` → `1,078`** and `block_cull` `reach=2` chunk from **`1,398` → `232`** (40 `lax.map` iterations vs 7). Across `ion`, 3×`mid`, 3×`contact`, `b_atom`, `bproj_c`, `_dyn_rows` loop trips jump from **~2,000 → ~12,200 iterations/state**, each issuing `dynamic_slice_in_dim`, a small 11.2M-candidate `_q_one` kernel (vs 67.1M), and two `dynamic_update_slice_in_dim` updates into 34 MB buffers.
   - **CSR double-indirection on all 10 atom calls/grid (`+0.25 to +0.45 s/state`, est.)**: Baseline read one contiguous 96-byte row `table[cid]` from a tiny L1/L2-resident dense atom table (`1.1 MB` focused, `21.2 MB` coarse at `B=16`). [maps.py:159-161](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L159-L161) replaces it with two gathers (`start[cid]`, `stop[cid]`) + an unpredicated third gather `order[min(kk, N-1)]` over all 24 slots (~80–85% empty) before `P[idx]`/`w[idx]`, and again must materialize or recompute `idx` across `argmin` in `_pick` ([maps.py:123](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L123)).

3. **Partial-batch serialization to `B = 1` via `partial_group` (`lax.cond(jnp.all(active), ..., lax.map(site, xs))`) — est. `+0.40 to +0.75 s/state`** ([maps.py:622-635](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L622-L635))
   - Across the 20 bench states (`1,611` sites in `111` batches, ~19/20 states have a partial tail batch averaging ~7.3 real sites, up to 15), whenever `not jnp.all(active)`, GPU falls into `partial_group` and executes `_maps_group(..., B=1)` **sequentially one site at a time** inside `lax.map`.
   - Per [DESIGN.md:41](DESIGN.md#L41), the A40 (84 SMs) saturates at `B = 8`; at `B = 1`, `argsort`, `searchsorted`, `nonzero`, `block_cull`, `contact`, `bproj`, and `model` grids are latency-/under-occupancy-bound (~3–4.5× slower per site than `B = 16`). Running ~7 tail sites sequentially costs ~1.1–1.5 s/state vs ~0.58 s/state for a batched tail (also explaining why `B8→B9` capping `B=1` atom chunk to 4096 added `+0.02 s/state` and why 5 small states in `C2-smoke` jumped `1.61 → 5.03 s/state`).

4. **`reach_mask_b` chunk shrunk 16× on GPU — est. `+0.35 to +0.65 s/state`** ([maps.py:96](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L96), [105](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L105), [563](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L563), [567-593](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L567-L593))
   - Baseline `reach_mask` was the single largest maps op (`0.84 s/state`). Because `GPU_REACH_BYTES = 256 MiB` divides by `B=16` **and** `64 B/candidate` (`268 MB / 1024 = 262,144` vs baseline `4,194,304`), `ch` dropped **16×**:
     - Focused/model (`O = 1,064`): **`3,942` → `246`** probes/chunk.
     - Coarse (`O = 32`): **`131,072` → `8,192`** probes/chunk.
   - Loop trips explode **16×** (~500 → **~7,800 `fori_loop` iterations/state**), multiplying loop-control/kernel-launch barriers, strided `pp[:, c]` slicing ([maps.py:589](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L589)), in-loop `grid_coords` recomputation ([maps.py:568](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L568)), and L2 thrash across sequential scatters into the 8.5 MB `mask` (> 6 MB L2). On GPU, XLA fuses `i0→idx→M→d→flat` so actual memory is ~4–8 B/candidate, not 64 B.

5. **Redundant `lax.cond` around `stage0` + outer `lax.map`/`lax.cond` barriers — est. `+0.05 to +0.15 s/state`** ([maps.py:450-452](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L450-L452), [632-636](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L632-L636))
   - On GPU, [maps.py:633](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L633) already guarantees `active` is all-`True` inside both `_maps_group(B=16)` and `_maps_group(B=1)`. Yet [maps.py:450-452](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L450-L452) unconditionally emits `lax.cond(jnp.all(active), V(stage0), lax.map(site0))` 6 times (3 grids × 2 widths). The `kConditional` barrier prevents cross-stage fusion/rematerialization of `G` (102 MB), `Gm` (102 MB), `flat` (34 MB), `pcls` (34 MB) into `qrows`/`stage1`, and bloats `maps_batch` HLO/compile size by ~2–3×.

6. **Model `_brute_chunk` 3× smaller (`21,845` vs `65,536`) — est. `+0.01 to +0.03 s/state`** ([maps.py:113-114](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L113-L114), [461](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L461)): minor (model is 6% of maps).

### 2. Why CPU Maps Did Not Speed Up (370 → 376 s/state) and What Dominates CPU Time

**What dominates CPU maps time:**
- **Raw candidate volume & ~80–90% wasted `-1` padding**: Per site, maps evaluates **~1.05B atom-cell candidates** (`~710M` focused + `~340M` coarse across 10 calls/grid, of which ~80–85% are `-1` slots since 4.01 Å cells hold ~1.5–5 atoms on average vs `Ka = 24`), **~310M `reach_mask` probe–offset pairs**, and **~160–310M probe-projection candidates** (~90–95% empty). That is **~1.5–1.7B 3D `sqrt` distances + indirect gathers + reductions per site** (~56B candidates/state across the 5-state CPU mix).
- **XLA-CPU materialization blowing past L2/L3 cache**: Unlike GPU, XLA-CPU does not fuse `gather(P[idx]) → q - pp → norm → argmin → take_along_axis` in registers; it materializes `kk`, `idx`, `pp`, `diff`, `sq`, `d` into memory on every chunk. On the 6-core Zen 4 (1 MB L2/core, 32 MB L3/CCD), the 230× CPU/A40 maps gap ([profile2.json:346](results/profile2.json#L346)) is dominated by 6–8 DRAM round-trips per candidate (~3.5–5.5 TB/state) plus poor multi-thread scaling (3.4–3.6× on 12 HTs, [bench.json:120](results/bench.json#L120)).

**Why Phase 1 gave no CPU speedup despite `B=4` (`-18.6%` in [profile2.json:630-644](results/profile2.json#L630-L644)) and padding skip (`-15–20%` est.):**
Phase 1 introduced ~+120–135 s/state of CPU regressions that completely cancelled the ~30–35% gain from `B=4` + padding skips:
1. **CSR reconstruction added 3–4 full-tile memory passes to the hottest loop (1.05B atom candidates/site)**: At `B = 4`, dense atom tables were only `0.28 MB` (focused, fits L2) and `5.3 MB` (coarse, fits L3) — a single contiguous 96-byte gather per cell. Replacing them with CSR ([maps.py:159-161](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L159-L161)) added `start[cid]`, `stop[cid]`, `kk`, `ok & (kk < stop)`, unpredicated `order[kk]`, and `where` on every atom query chunk. (Removing the dense *coarse probe* table saved the 15 GB OOM peak, but atom queries never touched it.)
2. **`64 MiB` / `32 MiB` tiles still spill Zen 4's 32 MB L3**: At `B = 4, Ka = 24`, `chunk = 539` (`1.40M` candidates/chunk) materializes `kk` (5.6 MB) + `idx` (5.6 MB) + `pp` (16.8 MB) + `diff` (16.8 MB) + `sq` (5.6–16.8 MB) + `d` (5.6 MB) ≈ **56–67 MB**, still >2× L3 — so lowering peak RAM from 20 GB to <1 GB yielded **zero L2/L3 cache hit benefit**.
3. **12×–32× more `fori_loop` chunks + per-chunk `lax.cond` barriers**: CPU `chunk` shrank 12× (`6,472 → 539` atoms, `1,398 → 116` cull) and `reach_mask_b` `ch` shrank 32× (`3,942 → 123` focused, `131,072 → 4,096` coarse), multiplying XLA-CPU Eigen thread-pool fork/join barriers (~40,000 loop iterations/state). Inside both loops, `lax.cond(jnp.all(cnt > 0), ...)` ([maps.py:367](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L367)) and `lax.cond(jnp.all(npreg > 0), ...)` ([maps.py:588](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L588)) are re-evaluated on every chunk and serialize partial groups (or any full group with a `cnt == 0` site) into 1-site `lax.map`.
4. **32-slot CSR slabs for probes** ([maps.py:134-158](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L134-L158)): per-slab `order[kk]` + `argmin` + 2× `take_along_axis` + `rank` passes offset the slot reduction over `Kp = 768`.

| Focused atoms | `729` | `24 / 48` | **1.1 MB** (2.2) | 0.56 MB | **0.28 MB** (0.56) | 10 calls |

### 4. Correctness and Parity Risks in the Current Code

1. **`fori_loop` dtype mismatch crash under `jax_enable_x64=True`** ([maps.py:140-157](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L140-L157), [372](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L372)): `init[1]` and `init[2]` are hardcoded `jnp.int32`, while `order = jnp.argsort(cid)` ([maps.py:68](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L68)), `slot`, `a = jnp.argmin(d, axis=1)`, and `rank` ([maps.py:154](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L154)) promote to `int64` when x64 is enabled (as `api.py` sets globally). Masked behind `with GEO.x32():` inside `pb.solve_state`, but any direct call to `maps_batch`/`query_min` after importing `api` crashes with a carry-dtype `TypeError`. Fix: cast `order` and `rank` explicitly to `jnp.int32`.
2. **Latent `B=1` vs `B=4` / `B=16` bitwise parity hazard on full and partial batches**:
   - **CPU** ([maps.py:367](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L367), [588](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L588)): `jnp.all(cnt > 0)` falls back to single-site `B=1` `tail` not only for padded groups, but also on **full 4-site groups** whenever one real site has `nbproj == 0` (common on convex model compounds). Because `C6` already proved LLVM changes f32 rounding between `B=1` and `B=4` on CPU ([opt1.json:2352-2401](results/opt1.json#L2352-L2401)), running `_q_one`/`_b_one`/`reach_mask_b` at `B=1` in `tail` risks a 1-ULP distance/boundary flip on unseen states or LLVM upgrades.
   - **GPU** ([maps.py:622-635](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L622-L635)): the same risk exists between `B=1` (`partial_group`) and `B=16` (`real_group`). Dropping `partial_group` eliminates it.
3. **Garbage outputs on padded sites in partial CPU groups** ([maps.py:445-456](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L445-L456), [467](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L467), [475-496](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L475-L496), [549](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L549)): `empty` initializes `pcls = 0` (`inb = True`) and `flat = 0` (not `N3`), so padded sites inside a partial CPU group output `ex = ey = ez = True` at 531,440 points, `ion[0] = True`, and non-zero `surf` (up to `1e4`) — inconsistent with `empty_maps` (`False`/`0.0`) and GPU `partial_group`, and fed into `estimate_specrad` ([pb.py:462](src/jaxpb/pb.py#L462)) before being masked by `valid`. Fix: set `empty["pcls"] = ones(..., 1)` and `empty["flat"] = full(..., N3)`, or mask `res` by `active`.
4. **Silent `XL_KA = 48` performance cliff & untested `K % 32 != 0` path** ([maps.py:134](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L134), [records.py:136](src/jaxpb/records.py#L136)): `XL_KA = 48 > 32` routes all XL atom queries through the 32-slot slab loop (math checks out, since `slot < K` masks `48..63` and `rank = (a//32)*48 + k*32 + a%32`, but [test_opt1.py:83](tests/test_opt1.py#L83) only tested `K = 64`). Dense atom tables remove this path entirely.
5. **Minor contract / hygiene issues**:
   - `active` inferred implicitly from `jnp.any(srad > 0, axis=1)` ([maps.py:615](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L615), [629](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L629), [649](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L649)): silently zeroes coarse/focused maps if a test/caller passes zero `srad`.
   - `jax.default_backend() == "cpu"` inside `@jax.jit` ([maps.py:101](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L101), [359](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L359), [581](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L581), [600](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L600), [616](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L616)): ignores `jax.default_device(cpu)` on GPU hosts.
   - `build_cells` omits explicit `stable=True` on `jnp.argsort(cid)` ([maps.py:68](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L68), cf. [geometry.py:109](src/jaxpb/geometry.py#L109)); relies on JAX's current default.
   - Dead `budget` parameter in `reach_mask_b` ([maps.py:556](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L556), [563](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L563)).

### 5. Ranked Fix Plan (Top 5)

1. **Fix GPU tile budgets & hoist `grid_coords`** ([maps.py:95-114](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L95-L114), [563](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L563), [568](src/jaxpb/maps.py#L568)): Use GPU fusion byte factors (~8 B/candidate) so GPU `reach_mask_b` runs at `ch = 1,971–3,942` (focused/model) / `65,536–131,072` (coarse), atom `_q_chunk` at `4,096–6,472` (`reach=1`) / `1,398` (`reach=2`), and `_brute_chunk` at `65,536`.
   - *Est. impact*: **A40 `-0.70 to -1.20 s/state`**, GPU `+0.25–0.55 GB`, zero parity risk.
